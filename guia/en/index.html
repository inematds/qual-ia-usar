In one sentence

This project compares ten AI models from three companies: Google's Gemini 4 (Neon, Argon, and Hydrogen), Anthropic's Claude (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku), and the OpenAI models Codex uses (Astra, Sol, and Luna). It brings together official pricing tables and the Agent Arena leaderboard, a public leaderboard that measures AI agents (AIs that carry out a task on their own, step by step) doing real work, checked on Nov 19, 2026. It's for people who pay for AI and want to choose without wasting money. You don't need anything to read it. To test it with your own task, all you need are the apps you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini), or the testing kit, which runs the same task across all the AIs and shows which one got more right while spending less.

In practice

How Nei chooses

The numbers tell you what it costs. The experience of using it tells you what it's worth. These are Nei's everyday choices.

🪶 Luna and Haiku: inexpensive for simple, long tasks

GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 cost pennies and got the simple task in our test right. They're good options for simple, long runs: many repeated steps, high volume, and work that doesn't require deep reasoning.

⭐ Terra and Sonnet: Nei's favorites

In Nei's tests, his favorites are GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Sonnet 5.5: they cost more than Luna and Haiku, but deliver good results more consistently. At OpenAI, the order from least to most expensive is Luna, Terra, Sol, and Astra. Terra isn't included in the tables above.

🔍 Neon as the critic

Nei currently uses Claude and Codex to review each other's work: models from different companies make different mistakes. Gemini 4 Neon can be that second set of eyes, an AI that checks and critiques, for anyone who only has Claude or only has Codex.

How to get started

Find out which AI costs less for your task

The leaderboard is a good starting point, but the right answer comes from your task. There are two options: one you do by hand with the apps you already subscribe to, and another where your AI sets up the test for you.

✋ Option 1: do it by hand with the subscriptions you already have

You don't need to install anything or create a new account. Don't subscribe to all three? They have free versions with limits that are enough for this test.

  1. Choose a real task from your work and decide in advance what "done right" means.
  2. Ask for the same task 3 times in each app, on a free or paid plan (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), always in a new conversation.
  3. Write down how many times it was correct without your help, out of 3.
  4. In the calculator above, enter the minutes you spend on each attempt (asking, reading, and checking) under "cost per attempt," along with the attempts and correct answers you noted. The lowest result uses the least of your time and usage limit.

🤖 Option 2: the testing kit runs your task on every AI you enable

You describe the task and what "done right" means. The kit sends the same task to each AI several times, an evaluator checks every answer, and it produces a report: how many were correct, how long it took, and how much each successful task cost. Your AI sets up and runs the kit.

1. Open your AI

Claude Code (included with a Claude subscription) or Codex (included with ChatGPT): these are versions of those AIs that work with files on your computer. Never used them? Stick with Option 1.

2. Paste the prompt below

It tells the AI to read the kit, format your task for it, and show you the plan without running anything yet.

3. Approve the test

Claude and GPT run through Claude Code and Codex subscriptions. For Gemini, or to use models outside a subscription, you'll need Gemini CLI (Google's program for running Gemini on your computer) or a pay-as-you-go account (the API) with a credit card. You approve which models to include, and it returns the report.

Read the "Which AI to use" project at https://github.com/inematds/qual-ia-usar (the README explains the testing kit in kit/testar.py).
I want to find out which AI has the lowest cost per successful task for one of my tasks: [describe the task and what "done right" means].
1. In plain English, tell me which 3 or 4 models are worth testing for this task and why.
2. Propose a small test: how many times to run each model and how much it should cost.
3. Don't call any model or spend anything until I say yes.
After I say yes, format the task for the kit, run the test, and show me the report.

The prompt tells the AI to show the cost and wait for your approval before spending anything.

Common questions

Before you choose

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You don't need anything to read the comparison. To test, use Option 1 with the apps. Option 2 requires Claude Code or Codex and, outside a subscription, an account with a credit card at each company.

I pay a monthly subscription, not by usage. Does this still help?

Token prices apply to people who pay as they go directly to the company (through the API). For subscribers, they can guide which model to use for each type of task. With a subscription, you don't pay per token, but a model that makes fewer mistakes also uses less of your limit.

So which AI is best?

For raw quality, Claude Fable 5.2 leads the leaderboard. For cost per task, Argon leads among models with a leaderboard score, until its price doubles on Dec 1. For your task, the answer comes from your test.

I already subscribe to ChatGPT. Is Claude or Gemini worth subscribing to?

It depends on your task. Follow Option 1 with the free versions: whichever one gets your task right most often is the one worth subscribing to.

How much does it cost?

The project and this guide are free. Testing the models costs whatever the companies charge, and the prompt tells the AI to show you the cost first.
